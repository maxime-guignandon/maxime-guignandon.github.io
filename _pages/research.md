I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at the École Normale Supérieure de Lyon, working in the ScAI team at the Laboratoire de Biologie et Modélisation de la Cellule (LBMC).

## PhD Research

### Interpretability for Kernel-Based Statistical Testing

Kernel-based statistical tests provide a powerful framework for detecting differences between distributions and identifying statistical dependencies. However, their interpretation can be challenging: a significant test result does not necessarily reveal which characteristics of the data are responsible for the observed difference.

My research aims to develop methods that make kernel-based statistical testing **more interpretable**, by identifying and quantifying the contribution of different features or observations to a test statistic.

The broader objective is to bridge the gap between the statistical power of kernel methods and the interpretability required to understand their conclusions.
